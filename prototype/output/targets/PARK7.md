## Why it's on the shortlist

Knocking down **PARK7** produces a broad, significant transcriptional effect in primary CD4+ T cells, and the gene is a plausible drug target linked to **ulcerative colitis**. It rose from raw-impact rank #73 to actionable rank #10 because the druggability + immune-genetics overlay favors it over higher-impact but non-actionable hits (e.g. core TCR machinery).
