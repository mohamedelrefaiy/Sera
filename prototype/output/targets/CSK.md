# CSK — candidate T-cell regulator

**Verdict: ✅ PROMOTE**  ·  actionable rank **#4** of the shortlist  ·  raw-impact rank #15

## Why it's on the shortlist

Knocking down **CSK** produces a broad, significant transcriptional effect in primary CD4+ T cells, and the gene is a plausible drug target linked to **systemic lupus erythematosus**. It rose from raw-impact rank #15 to actionable rank #4 because the druggability + immune-genetics overlay favors it over higher-impact but non-actionable hits (e.g. core TCR machinery).
