# STAT6 — candidate T-cell regulator

**Verdict: ✅ PROMOTE**  ·  actionable rank **#12**  ·  raw-impact rank #248

## Why it's on the shortlist

Knocking down **STAT6** produces a broad, significant transcriptional effect in primary CD4+ T cells, and the gene is a plausible drug target linked to **asthma**. It rose from raw-impact rank #248 to actionable rank #12 because the druggability + immune-genetics overlay favors it over higher-impact but non-actionable hits (e.g. core TCR machinery).

## Independent corroboration (held-out)

Not a significant hit in the held-out screens (Schmidt 2022 / Freimer 2022). This is **not disqualifying** — those screens cover a different gene set and readouts; STAT6's independent support here is clinical/genetic (Open Targets), not screen-based.

## What would refute this

- the effect does not reproduce in all three conditions — the context it acts in matters; pick the matching stimulation state for validation
- no held-out screen hit — an arrayed knockdown in a fresh donor is the cheapest disconfirming experiment

## Suggested validation

- **Perturbation:** CRISPRi knockdown of STAT6 (2 guides) in primary CD4+ T cells.
- **Context:** stimulated (Stim8hr).
- **Readout:** activation/polarization program (IL2 / IFNG); compare to non-targeting control.
- **Predicted direction:** matches the screen's on-target effect (see evidence table).
