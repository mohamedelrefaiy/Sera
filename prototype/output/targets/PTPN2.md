# PTPN2 — candidate T-cell regulator

> 🔦 **Demo spotlight.** Flagged with no knowledge of the clinic — PTPN2 is a druggable phosphatase brake on T-cell activation. Its inhibitor **ABBV-CLS-484** is in **Phase 1** ([NCT04777994](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04777994), recruiting). We surfaced the target blind; the clinic agrees.

**Verdict: ✅ PROMOTE**  ·  actionable rank **#42**  ·  raw-impact rank #812

## Why it's on the shortlist

Knocking down **PTPN2** produces a broad, significant transcriptional effect in primary CD4+ T cells, and the gene is a plausible drug target linked to **type 1 diabetes mellitus**. It rose from raw-impact rank #812 to actionable rank #42 because the druggability + immune-genetics overlay favors it over higher-impact but non-actionable hits (e.g. core TCR machinery).
