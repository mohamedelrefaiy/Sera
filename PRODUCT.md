# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Experimental life scientists evaluating CRISPR screen hits while planning follow-up work. They need to understand what was measured, decide whether a target should advance, and translate uncertain cross-assay evidence into a defensible bench experiment.

## Product Purpose

Sera reconciles transcript and protein evidence into an inspectable scientific decision. Success means a researcher can quickly explain the call, see its limitations, and leave with the experiment most likely to change the decision.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and scientifically candid. The product should feel like a beautifully made research instrument: focused enough for repeated laboratory use and distinctive enough to be remembered after a demonstration.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI chat client, a decorative SaaS dashboard, or a collection of interchangeable cards. Avoid animation without experimental meaning, empty side panels, repeated summaries, unexplained statistics, and controls that interrupt the scientific reading flow.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the experimental decision, then show how it was earned.
2. Preserve the agent narrative as the connective scientific explanation.
3. Separate measurement, interpretation, and uncertainty explicitly.
4. Convert uncertainty into a concrete next experiment.
5. Reveal supporting detail progressively without hiding provenance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Never encode assays, significance, or decisions by color alone. Maintain keyboard-visible focus, usable 32-pixel minimum targets, readable scientific line lengths, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
